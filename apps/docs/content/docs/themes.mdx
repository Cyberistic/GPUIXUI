## Reading the audit

One line per finding, with the ratio and the floor it had to reach:
`mutedForeground on muted — 3.90:1 (needs 4.5:1)`. **Unmeasurable** means the
contrast cannot be computed, because a colour is translucent or a wash is
composited, rather than that it passed; the prefix names the report it came from.

A switch is measured on its box as well as its colours:
`switch height — 22 cannot hold a knob that needs 32`, and
`switch width — 0 cannot be laid out (needs a size greater than zero)`.

Edits are stored per mode over the platform style the sidebar selected, so a
switch of platform re-bases the audit while your edits stay. **Reset** clears
them and **Copy theme** prints the resolved module. A browser that refuses site
storage keeps the editor and the URL, because the edit is also in the link.
