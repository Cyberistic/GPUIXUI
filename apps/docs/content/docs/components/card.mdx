`Card` groups related content on one surface. It is composition only: it has no
state, does not focus, scroll, dismiss or trap anything, and never wraps an
overlay. Anything interactive inside a card is a control you put there.

## Usage

```tsx
import {
  Card, CardContent, CardDescription, CardFooter, CardHeader, CardTitle,
} from "@gpuixui/components/card";

<Card>
  <CardHeader>
    <CardTitle>Pro plan</CardTitle>
    <CardDescription>Everything in Starter, plus seats and invoicing.</CardDescription>
  </CardHeader>
  <CardContent>{/* rows, a form, a chart */}</CardContent>
  <CardFooter>{/* actions */}</CardFooter>
</Card>;
```

Only `Card` is required. The parts are `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`,
`CardDescription`, `CardContent` and `CardFooter`; drop the ones you do not need.

## Variants

| `variant` | Surface |
| --------- | ------- |
| `outline` (default) | 1px `border` and the `card` fill |
| `elevated` | the `card` fill and the `md` elevation shadow |
| `plain` | the `card` fill only, for a card inside another surface |

## Sizes

No size prop: spacing, radius and type come from the theme. A consumer `style`
is merged last, so `style={{ maxWidth: 420 }}` constrains the surface without a
fork.

## States

None. A card is never focusable, hoverable or disabled, and is not a landmark.
`CardTitle` is the level-3 heading that names it.

## Keyboard and focus

Not focusable. Tab order belongs to the controls inside the content.

## Theming

All colour, spacing, radius, elevation and type come from the theme
(`colors.card`, `colors.border`, `spacing.*`, `radius.lg`, `elevation.md`,
`textStyle("h3")` / `textStyle("body")`). There are no literal colours or sizes.

## Accessibility and limitations

- `CardTitle` renders a `text` node with `role="heading"` and `aria-level={3}`.
- A card contributes no role of its own; its accessible name comes from its
  heading, not a wrapper label.
- The card does not clip a scrollable body: `overflow` is hidden on the surface,
  so a scroller belongs in `CardContent` with its own height.
- No live scene ships for this component yet; the example and source below are real.
