## Usage

```tsx
import {
  Popover,
  PopoverContent,
  PopoverTrigger,
} from "@gpuixui/components/popover";

<Popover>
  <PopoverTrigger variant="secondary">Columns</PopoverTrigger>
  <PopoverContent side="bottom" align="start">
    <ColumnToggles />
  </PopoverContent>
</Popover>;
```

## Sides

<ComponentPreview name="popover-sides" />

`side` names the edge the surface opens on and `sideOffset` the gap to the
trigger (6px by default): `top`, `right`, `bottom` (the default) or `left`. The
requested side is kept unless it has **no room at all** for the surface; then it
flips to the opposite side, and the roomier one wins when neither fits.

## Collision

<ComponentPreview name="popover-collision" />

A surface that overhangs the window keeps its side and slides back by
`collisionPadding` (8px); the trigger does not move, and no sibling does either —
the surface is an anchored layer, out of the layout flow.

## Limitations

- `aria-expanded` is on the trigger.
- **No `aria-haspopup`, `aria-controls` or `aria-labelledby`** in `@gpuix/native@0.11.0`; the link between trigger and surface is positional, not semantic.
- Positioning is the runtime's anchored layer: no arrow, no custom anchor element and no backdrop — a popover is non-modal, so it paints no veil and does not blur the page. It measures its **containing block** (the popover's positioning root), so a `left`/`right` popover belongs in a shrink-to-fit container: in a block that stretches its children the root is wider than the trigger.

## Motion

The panel fades and travels 8px out from the side it opens on, on the `normal`
level (200 ms); `MotionProvider preference="reduced"` paints the resting frame at
once. A leaving panel stays painted for its exit and takes no presses.

## Theming

The surface paints the theme's `elevation.lg` beside its 1px `foreground` ring,
so a brand's lift and edge both show. Its corner is the recipe's fixed **24px**
(`rounded-3xl`): the platform switch repaints the roles and leaves the corner
alone, while the preview follows the platform radius. The preview is the
approximation; the shipped box is the fixed one.
