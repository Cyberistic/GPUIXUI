## Composition

`Agent` in `@gpuixui/blocks/agent`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
the sessions' `SourceList`, `Avatar` turns, an `Input` composer with `Send`, and a changed-files inspector. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
