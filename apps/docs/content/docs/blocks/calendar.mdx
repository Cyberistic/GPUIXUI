## Composition

`CalendarBlock` in `@gpuixui/blocks/calendar`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
calendar `Switch`es, ‹ Today › and a Day/Week/Month `SegmentedControl`, and events laid out by the pure `layoutDay` rule. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
