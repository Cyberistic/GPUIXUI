A sign-in recipe: a `Card` with email and password fields, a `Checkbox`, and a
primary action in the footer. Copy the recipe; it is composed from the library,
not a new component.

<BlockPreview name="login-01" />

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Card`, `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CardDescription`, `CardContent` and
  `CardFooter` from `@gpuixui/components/card`
- `Button`, `Checkbox`, `Input`, `Label`, `Separator`, `Stack` and `Text` from
  `@gpuixui/primitives`

Both are workspace packages already on the component page; the recipe adds no
new dependency. Wire `signIn` to your own handler and `fullWidth` keeps the
primary action on the card's edge.
