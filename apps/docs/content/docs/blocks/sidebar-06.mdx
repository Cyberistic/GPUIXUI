The whole docs tree folded into four parent rows: a row opens its pages as a
menu beside the panel, and the footer carries shadcn's newsletter card.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-06" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider`, `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and `SidebarRail` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `DropdownMenu`, `DropdownMenuTrigger`, `DropdownMenuContent` and
  `DropdownMenuItem` for the section menus
- `SidebarFooter` for the newsletter card, `SidebarInput` for the email field
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`; `Stack` and `Text` for the copy
- `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

The row is the menu trigger, so a press anywhere on the label opens the pages;
the ellipsis only says the row has more behind it. The card in the footer is the
panel's own column, not a floating surface, and the menu opens to the right of
the panel on a wide window.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
