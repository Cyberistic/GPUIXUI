A Messages-window recipe: the conversation list on the left, the open thread on
the right and the composer beneath it. The same block is drawn in three
platform dresses so the deck on the landing page can show macOS, Windows 11 and
Linux side by side; the preview below is the macOS one. Copy the recipe; it is
composed from the library, not a new component.

<BlockPreview name="conversations-01" />

The preview is interactive: click a conversation to swap the thread (Gon,
Kurapika and Bisky have a short one; the rest open on an empty state), type in
the composer and press Enter to append an outgoing bubble, and type in the
search field to filter the list by name or preview. The window keeps its height,
so a longer thread scrolls its own log instead of moving the window; below 760px
of viewport the right pane is hidden and the list is the whole window, as
Messages does on a narrow screen.

## The three dresses

`ConversationWindow` takes `platform` and changes only the skin:

- **macOS** — white window, 12px radius, traffic lights on the left, a 330px
  grey list and a filled blue selected row; green outgoing bubbles with 18px
  corners. The sidebar toggle sits at the list's top-right, compose at the
  thread's top-left, video and phone at its top-right.
- **Windows 11** — light Fluent window, 8px radius, `− □ ×` on the right, a 72px
  icon rail with a blue selected message icon, a white list with a light blue
  selected row, and blue outgoing bubbles. The composer's blue send is a circle.
- **Linux (Breeze dark)** — dark window, 8px radius, traffic lights on the left
  *and* `− □ ×` on the right, a teal-selected message icon in the rail, a teal
  selected row and blue outgoing bubbles.

The literals become roles — `success`, `primary`, `info`, `selected`,
`success-soft`, `muted` — so every dress works in both the light and the dark
palette. The double tick is `info` in all three.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Avatar` from `@gpuixui/components/avatar`
- `ScrollArea`, `Stack`, `Text` and `Input` from `@gpuixui/primitives`
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`

The window chrome — traffic lights, window controls, the rail, the compose and
call glyphs — is the app's own, drawn as the block's browser reproduction.
`search`, `select` and `send` are the only handlers to wire; the list filter and
the sent bubble are the app's state, so a new conversation is a datum, not a
second render path.
