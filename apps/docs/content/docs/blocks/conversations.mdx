A Messages-window recipe: the conversation list on the left, the open thread on
the right and the composer beneath it. The same block is drawn in four
platform dresses so the deck on the landing page can show macOS, Windows 11,
Linux and Omarchy side by side; the preview below is the macOS one. Copy the
recipe; it is composed from the library, not a new component.

<BlockPreview name="conversations-01" />

The preview is interactive: click a conversation to swap the thread (Gon,
Kurapika and Bisky have a short one; the rest open on an empty state), type in
the composer and press Enter to append an outgoing bubble, and type in the
search field to filter the list by name or preview. The window keeps its height,
so a longer thread scrolls its own log instead of moving the window; below 760px
of viewport the right pane is hidden and the list is the whole window, as
Messages does on a narrow screen.

## The four dresses

`ConversationWindow` takes `platform` and changes only the skin. **The control
set is identical across the four**: a compose glyph at the thread's top left,
video and phone at its top right, a search field and selectable rows, and a
composer of `+`, the field, emoji, attach and send.

- **macOS** — white window, 12px radius, traffic lights on the left (and no
  window glyphs), a 330px grey list and a filled blue selected row; green
  outgoing bubbles with 18px corners. A sidebar toggle sits at the list's
  top-right, next to the search field.
- **Windows 11** — light Fluent window, 8px radius, `− □ ×` on the right, a 72px
  icon rail with a blue selected message icon, a white list with a light blue
  selected row, and blue outgoing bubbles.
- **Linux (Breeze dark)** — dark window, 8px radius, `− □ ×` on the right, a
  teal-selected message icon in the rail, a teal selected row and blue outgoing
  bubbles.
- **Omarchy** — the Linux rail and window in the desktop's own Tokyo Night
  look: near-square corners, the theme's `selected` row and a `primary`
  outgoing bubble, so it follows the palette an Omarchy desktop stages.

The rail is the Windows, Linux and Omarchy skin's replacement for the macOS
sidebar toggle, not a different feature set. The literals become roles —
`success`, `primary`, `info`, `selected`, `success-soft`, `muted` — so every
dress works in both the light and the dark palette. The double tick is `info` in
all four.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Avatar` from `@gpuixui/components/avatar`
- `ScrollArea`, `Stack`, `Text` and `Input` from `@gpuixui/primitives`
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`

The window chrome — traffic lights or window glyphs, the rail, the compose, call,
emoji, attach and send glyphs — is the app's own, drawn as the block's browser
reproduction. `search`, `select` and `send` are the only handlers to wire; the
list filter and the sent bubble are the app's state, so a new conversation is a
datum, not a second render path.
