The window a guild keeps open: the vault's branches down the left, the docket
in front, how much renown the fortnight brought, which stage each dossier sits
in, what is staged, what a missing fact holds, and the shape of the last eight
nights.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Button` from `@gpuixui/primitives` for the four view pills and the stage
  filter
- `Card` from `@gpuixui/components/card` for the five readings, the six stage
  cells and the dossier cards
- `Badge` from `@gpuixui/components/badge` for the bank, rank and length chips
- `Text` from `@gpuixui/primitives` for the `caption` labels and the `h3`
  numbers
- `Sidebar` from `@gpuixui/components/sidebar` for the rail's panel, its group
  and its `WanTo` footer
- `FileTree` from `@gpuixui/files` for the vault's branches, their fold and
  the one current note
- `Breadcrumb` from `@gpuixui/components/breadcrumb` for the trail above the
  docket

The window's own chrome is drawn to the reference: the traffic lights and the
six-note tab strip have no shipped part, so they are the app's frame rather
than a component drawing.

The window's chrome is drawn to the reference: the title bar's six notes, the
vault tree's branch structure and its one current row, and the breadcrumb. The
rail rows are the block's own — a tree row is a disclosure and an icon on one
line, not a `Button`.

The chart is drawn, not shipped: the runtime has no chart host, so the block
paints two polylines over the same night list the axis labels pick.

## What a reader can change

- **The stage filter** narrows the strip to the open stages or to the verified
  one; `all` keeps every stage.
- **A day on the chart** picks the night whose two figures the plot labels.
