Each docs section is a row that opens its pages beneath itself. The second
section lands open and the rest wait behind a plus, so the panel starts at the
size of its headings.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-05" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider`, `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and `SidebarRail` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `Collapsible` and `CollapsibleContent` per section, with the row as its
  trigger
- `SidebarMenuSub`, `SidebarMenuSubItem` and `SidebarMenuSubButton` for the
  pages
- `SidebarInput` for the search field, `Icon` for the plus and minus marks
- `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

Only the open section raises its own disclosure, so a reader can tell which
section the pages belong to. The closed sections are still full rows with a
plus, never an empty box. The panel keeps its rail.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
