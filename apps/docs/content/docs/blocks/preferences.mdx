## Composition

`Preferences` in `@gpuixui/blocks/preferences`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
a `SearchInput` over the panes' `SourceList`, and `InsetGroup` rows of `Switch`, `SegmentedControl` and `Select`. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
