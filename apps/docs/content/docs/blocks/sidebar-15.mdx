Both edges at once: the workspace and its favorites on the left, the project in
the middle and the calendar on the right, all in one provider.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-15" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider` with three children: a left `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and a
  right `Sidebar collapsible="none" side="right"`
- `SidebarRail` on the left panel only
- `DatePicker` and `DatePickerCalendar` in the right panel
- `SidebarUserMenu`, `SidebarSeparator` and the calendar groups in the right
  panel; the account sits in its header
- `Breadcrumb`, `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` for the page

## Notes

The right panel is `collapsible="none"` so the middle page never pays for a
second gap, and it declares `side="right"` itself. The left panel keeps the
icon collapse from block 07. The page is the workspace's open project, with its
task list; the right panel's month is the same drawn October 2024.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
