The sectioned sidebar with each heading acting as its own disclosure, over a
page long enough that the header has something to stick above.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-02" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider`, `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and `SidebarRail` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `Collapsible`, `CollapsibleTrigger` and `CollapsibleContent` around each
  `SidebarGroup`
- `SidebarMenuButton` for the rows, `SidebarInput` for the search field
- `Breadcrumb` and its parts for the sticky header
- `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

A section that is closed keeps its heading row, so the panel never paints an
empty group. The sticky header is the `SidebarInset`'s own header; nothing in
the sidebar needs to know the page scrolled. The body lists every topic in the
nav so the scroll is real, not a spacer.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
