A mail-client recipe: a folder rail with favorites and accounts, a toolbar over
the message list, a reading pane for the open thread and a contact card for its
sender. Copy the recipe; it is composed from the library, not a new component.

<BlockPreview name="email-inbox-01" />

The preview is interactive: click a message to repaint the reading pane with its
subject, sender and body; the Primary / Promotions / Social tabs filter the list
(Social is empty on this data, so the list says so instead of going blank); the
toolbar's Reply action focuses the reply box, and the contact card switches
between History and Attachments. The toolbar wraps at narrow widths, and the
contact rail is dropped below 1120px of the block's own width — the viewport's
1100px breakpoint does the same — so neither the list nor the reading pane is
clipped to make room for it.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Avatar` from `@gpuixui/components/avatar`, `Badge` from
  `@gpuixui/components/badge`, `Tabs` and its parts from
  `@gpuixui/components/tabs`, `EmptyState` from
  `@gpuixui/components/empty-state`
- `Button`, `IconButton`, `ScrollArea`, `Stack`, `Text` and `TextArea` from
  `@gpuixui/primitives`
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`

A provider's chip (`Promotion`) is a `Badge` in the success tone; the unread dot
is the primary role. The message's `category` is the filter the tabs read, so a
new tab is a datum, not a second list. The reply box is a `TextArea`; wire
`send`, `archive`, `snooze` and the rest of the toolbar to your own handlers.
