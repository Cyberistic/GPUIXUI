## Composition

`Music` in `@gpuixui/blocks/music`:

- the album art is a two-stop gradient picked per track, drawn here;
- the waveform is `waveform(seed, bars)` — a seeded shape, so the same track
  draws the same bars on every render (tested, including its range);
- the transport is a press target that toggles play and pause; the queue's rows
  are press targets that make the row the playing track, changing the art, the
  title, the waveform and the time together.

Nothing streams: the times are the tracks' own lengths and the scrubber is the
position, not a clock.
