Block 03's docs tree moved to the right edge: the page keeps the lead, the
contents panel sits against the far edge, and the page header holds the toggle.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-14" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider` with `SidebarInset` before `<Sidebar side="right">` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarGroup`, `SidebarGroupLabel`, `SidebarMenu`, `SidebarMenuButton`,
  `SidebarMenuSub` and `SidebarMenuSubButton` for the contents
- `SidebarRail` on the panel's inner edge
- `SidebarTrigger` in the inset header's trailing corner
- `Breadcrumb`, `Stack` and `Text` for the page

## Notes

`side="right"` is passed on the panel itself, so the inset keeps its full width
and the gap moves to the right. The trigger's icon names the edge it will
close. At a narrow width the panel drops to the icon rail with the rest of the
family.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
