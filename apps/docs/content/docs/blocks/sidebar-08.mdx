The inset variant: the panel is a lifted card on the window's own surface, the
page is a second rounded sheet, and secondary links sit at the bottom of the
panel.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-08" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider variant="inset"` and `Sidebar variant="inset"` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarHeader`, `SidebarContent`, `SidebarFooter` and the platform/project
  groups
- `Collapsible`, `SidebarMenuAction` and `SidebarMenuSub` for the sections that
  open
- `SidebarGroup style={{ marginTop: "auto" }}` for Support and Feedback
- `SidebarUserMenu` in the footer; `Icon` for the glyphs
- `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

The provider declares the variant once; the panel and the inset both read it.
The panel keeps its gutter on three sides and the sheet takes the rest of the
width, which is the one shape the shell cannot express with a parent selector.
The footer is an ordinary menu row, so icon collapse would show the avatar.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
