## Composition

`Launcher` in `@gpuixui/blocks/launcher`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
an `Input`, results ranked by prefix within their group, ↑/↓ and ↵, on the `hud` material — a centred panel until overlay windows (plan 706) land. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
