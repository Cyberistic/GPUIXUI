## Composition

- `Badge` from `@gpuixui/components/badge`, `Card` and its parts from
  `@gpuixui/components/card`, `SearchInput` from `@gpuixui/components/search-input`
- `BarChart`, `DonutChart` and `StackedBar` from `@gpuixui/data`
- `Box`, `Button`, `Progress`, `Stack` and `Text` from `@gpuixui/primitives`
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`

## Charts

`BarChart`, `DonutChart` and `StackedBar` are documented on [Charts](/data-display/charts). The runtime paints an `<svg source>` as one tint, so a multi-series chart is one stacked element per colour.

The preview is a browser reproduction, stacked like the recipe: its marks are drawn by hand in `--series-1` to `--series-5`.
