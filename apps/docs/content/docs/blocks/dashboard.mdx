A dashboard recipe for the **Hunter Association** that composes the most parts
of a window: a `Card` grid of stats, `Progress` rows for projects and apps,
`Badge` deltas, a `SearchInput`, `Icon` glyphs, and the `Stack`/`Text` layout
for the sidebar and top bar.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Badge` from `@gpuixui/components/badge`, `Card` and its parts from
  `@gpuixui/components/card`, `SearchInput` from `@gpuixui/components/search-input`
- `BarChart`, `DonutChart` and `StackedBar` from `@gpuixui/data`
- `Box`, `Button`, `Progress`, `Stack` and `Text` from `@gpuixui/primitives`
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons` — the nav's four rows are the glyphs the set
  carries (`grid`, `folder`, `clock`, `settings`); it has no globe or report mark

## Charts

The three charts are the package's: `BarChart` for the day's hours,
`DonutChart` for the token split and `StackedBar` for the hourly series. Each
series is one `<svg>` layer tinted through `style.color`, because the runtime
paints an `<svg source>` as **one tint** — measured by the playground's
`content-surfaces`, a shape's own `fill` never reaches the frame, so a
multi-series chart is one stacked element per colour. The colours are the theme's
categorical [data series](https://github.com/Cyberistic/GPUIXUI/blob/master/docs/tokens.md#data-series), never a palette of the
recipe's own. The parts are documented on
[Charts](/data-display/charts).

The preview above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette, because a browser cannot mount the native components: its bars, donut
and stacked columns are drawn by hand in the theme's series, read through
`--series-1` to `--series-5`, which is why the platform and look switch repaints
the marks and every legend swatch together.
