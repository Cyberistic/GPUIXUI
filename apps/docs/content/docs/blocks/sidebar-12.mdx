The calendar sidebar: the account, an inline month, three calendar lists with
their checked filters, and New Calendar in the footer.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-12" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider`, `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and `SidebarRail` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `DatePicker` and `DatePickerCalendar` for the inline month
- `SidebarGroup`, `SidebarGroupContent`, `SidebarMenu` and `SidebarMenuButton`
  for the calendar lists
- `SidebarSeparator` between the month and the lists
- `SidebarUserMenu` in the header, `SidebarMenuButton` for New Calendar
- `Breadcrumb`, `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` for the page

## Notes

`DatePicker` supplies the month arithmetic and selection; `DatePickerCalendar`
paints the grid inline, inside the panel, with `today` and `defaultValue` fixed
so the block paints October 2024 on every run. The page beside it is the month
itself: the 5×4 grid carries the day numbers and four events.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
