shadcn's settings dialog: the sections as a sidebar inside the modal, the
current section named in a breadcrumb, and its settings in the content column.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-13" />

## Composition

- `Dialog`, `DialogTrigger` and `DialogContent` from `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarProvider` and `Sidebar collapsible="none"` inside the content
- `SidebarContent`, `SidebarGroup`, `SidebarMenu` and `SidebarMenuButton` for the
  twelve sections, with **Messages & media** active
- `DialogClose` for the modal's close mark
- `Breadcrumb` and its parts for `Settings / Messages & media`
- `Stack` and `Text` for the section body

## Notes

The dialog is drawn open, as shadcn's block opens it on mount; the recipe's
`defaultOpen` reproduces that state without a press. The sidebar keeps
`collapsible="none"` so the modal's sections do not grow a rail or a second
toggle, and the content column's header carries the close mark rather than a
second trigger.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
