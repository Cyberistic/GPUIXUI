## Composition

`Welcome` in `@gpuixui/blocks/welcome`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
a compact `Window` with no sidebar, `Icon` feature rows and two `Button`s; Import… is the app's callback to the system picker. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
