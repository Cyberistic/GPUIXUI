The workspace sidebar on shadcn's icon collapse: the team switcher, four
platform sections that open in place, the project list with its row actions and
the account in the footer.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-07" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider collapsible="icon"` and `Sidebar` from `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarHeader` with a `DropdownMenu` team switcher
- `Collapsible` around each platform section, with `SidebarMenuSub` for its pages
- `SidebarMenuAction` for the project dots, `SidebarUserMenu` for the footer
- `Icon` from `@gpuixui/icons`; `Stack` and `Text` for the switcher
- `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

`collapsible="icon"` is the block's contract: the panel is 256px open and 48px
collapsed, the labels clip to icons, and every row's `tooltip` is what icon mode
shows. The panel starts open, so the screen paints the full labels; the rail and
⌘/Ctrl+B flip it. The **Playground** section starts open because it is the
current page.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
