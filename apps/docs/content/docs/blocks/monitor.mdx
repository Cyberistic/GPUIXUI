## Composition

`Monitor` in `@gpuixui/blocks/monitor`:

- two temperature cards, each a big value over a `Sparkline` of the window —
  `@gpuixui/data`'s chart, not a drawn path;
- one bar per core, drawn from its value and the theme's accent and muted roles;
- memory as a `DonutChart` with its legend;
- a window control (1 m · 5 m · 15 m) that re-samples both sparklines and the
  values. `samples(seed, base, count)` is seeded, so a window is the same shape
  on every render (tested, including its band).

Nothing polls and nothing claims to read the machine: the samples are the
block's data, and the window control is the interaction.
