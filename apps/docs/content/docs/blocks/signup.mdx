A registration recipe: a `Card` with four fields, a `Checkbox` for the terms of
service, a primary action and a footer that opens sign-in. Copy the recipe; it
is composed from the library, not a new component.

<BlockPreview name="signup-01" />

The preview is interactive: submit with a missing field, a short password,
mismatched passwords or the terms unchecked to see each error, or fill the form
in to watch the button go busy and the account resolve. The status row is
reserved, so the card never moves between states.

## Composition

The block composes:

- `Card`, `CardHeader`, `CardTitle`, `CardDescription`, `CardContent` and
  `CardFooter` from `@gpuixui/components/card`
- `Button`, `Checkbox`, `Input`, `Label`, `Stack` and `Text` from
  `@gpuixui/primitives`

Both are workspace packages already on the component page; the recipe adds no
new dependency. `Checkbox` renders its label beside the control, and
`Button`'s `loading` prop is the busy state. Wire `createAccount` and
`openSignIn`, and keep the password rules in one place on the way in.
