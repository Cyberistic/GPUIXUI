## Composition

`MenuBar` in `@gpuixui/blocks/menubar`, inside the shell's native window where it has one:
`Switch` rows driving the status line, a `Progress` and two ghost `Button`s, on the `popover` material — in its own window until the tray (plan 702) and overlay windows (706) land. Its rules are pure and tested in `examples/blocks/src/mac-blocks.logic.test.ts`,
and its primary interaction runs on the real renderer in `src/mac-blocks.test.tsx`.
