Block 03's tree on the floating variant: the panel is a rounded, lifted box
inside the window's gutter rather than a rail against the content.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-04" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider variant="floating"` and `Sidebar variant="floating"` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarMenu`, `SidebarMenuItem`, `SidebarMenuButton`, `SidebarMenuSub`,
  `SidebarMenuSubItem` and `SidebarMenuSubButton` for the tree
- `SidebarHeader` for the brand, `SidebarInset` for the page
- `Stack`, `Text` and `Separator` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

The variant is declared once on the provider: the panel keeps the 8px gutter
and the inset sits flush. The block has no rail — shadcn's floating recipe
toggles from the page header instead, so the panel's outer edge stays clean.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
