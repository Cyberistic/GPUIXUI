## Composition

`Podcast` in `@gpuixui/blocks/podcast`:

- the artwork is the caller's `cover` and it fills the window; a host without one
  gets the same poster in a two-stop field, because cover art is not ours to
  ship. The type sits on a scrim rather than the theme's ink: a photograph over
  the whole window is not a theme surface, so the block declares its own white;
- the waveform is `waveform(seed, bars)` — a seeded shape, so the same episode
  draws the same bars on every render (tested, including its range);
- the transport is a press target that toggles play and pause; the season's rows
  are press targets that make the row the playing episode, changing the title,
  the waveform and the time together.

Nothing streams: the times are the episodes' own lengths and the scrubber is a
position, not a clock.
