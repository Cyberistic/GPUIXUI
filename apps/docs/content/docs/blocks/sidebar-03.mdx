A docs tree where the section is the row and its pages hang beneath it, with no
labels and no disclosure: everything the sidebar can reach is on screen.

<BlockPreview name="sidebar-03" />

## Composition

- `SidebarProvider`, `Sidebar`, `SidebarInset` and `SidebarRail` from
  `@gpuixui/components`
- `SidebarMenu`, `SidebarMenuItem` and `SidebarMenuButton` for the sections
- `SidebarMenuSub`, `SidebarMenuSubItem` and `SidebarMenuSubButton` for the
  pages, with `isActive` on **Data Fetching**
- `SidebarMenuButton size="lg"` for the brand row
- `Stack` and `Text` from `@gpuixui/primitives`

## Notes

The sub list is a child of its section's `SidebarMenuItem`, so a long section
grows its row rather than a second list. `SidebarMenuSub` keeps the vertical
rule and the 14px indent; icon mode hides the sub lists with the labels.

The screen above is a browser reproduction of the recipe's geometry and
palette; the disclosure under it holds the `@gpuixui/*` source to copy.
