---
title: Data display
description: Tables, selection, and virtualised data presentation.
---

Data presentation covers tables, sortable and selectable rows, column
configuration, resizable columns, filtering controls, tree views and key-value
displays. Virtualisation is the interesting case: it is only offered where GPUix
can actually render it, and the pages record the measured behaviour rather than
promising it.

These live in `@gpuixui/data-grid`. They depend on `@gpuixui/components` and are
verified in the native playground before their inventory row moves to `done`.
