Each reference has a record in [`LICENSES/`](https://github.com/Cyberistic/GPUIXUI/tree/master/LICENSES) saying its licence, what was ported and what was not. Notices that redistribution must carry live beside the port ([`packages/motion/NOTICE`](https://github.com/Cyberistic/GPUIXUI/blob/master/packages/motion/NOTICE)). No logo or trademark is used.

If you reimplement behaviour from these projects, read their licence and preserve their notice.
