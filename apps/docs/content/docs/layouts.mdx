---
title: Layouts
description: Stacks, boxes, separators and the layout primitives.
---

The layout primitives are the foundation every other component is built from:
horizontal and vertical stacks, boxes, separators, spacers and scrollable
containers. They map onto GPUix's flexbox layout rather than a browser's.

These live in `@gpuixui/primitives` and are covered in the
[component index](/docs/components). Their pages carry the honest GPUix support
status and a link to the native playground.
