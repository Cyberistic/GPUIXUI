## Findings

1. **Notification centre's open row is a state-precedence bug.** `notification-centre.tsx`
   paints the open item with `accent` (the hover wash), replaces that fill with
   `hover`/`active` while the pointer is over it, and paints `focusRing` whenever
   the row is open rather than when it holds focus. The open row therefore loses
   its only persistent cue under the pointer, looks focused while it is not, and
   uses a transient role where the design system names `selected` for a persistent
   row. Recorded cross-team in `TODO.md`; no token change is needed — `selected`,
   `selectedForeground` and the existing pairs already carry it.
2. **The disabled dim has a second fraction.** The TagInput chip dims at `0.6`
   (`tag-input.tsx:286`) and InlineEdit at `0.5` (`inline-edit.tsx:242`). The
   existing cross-team row named only `opacity: 0.5`; it now names both.
3. **No new pair is justified.** The probes over text-on-fill, ring-on-fill and
   edge-on-fill combinations found no shipped composition below a floor that is
   not already a recorded consumer row. Adding a pair that only blesses a
   composition no consistent palette fails is the manufacturing the plan-604 and
   plan-609 audits warn against.
