# tokens-theme — audit after plan 636 (2026-10-19, thirteenth tick)

## Scope

Plan 636 closed the Toast's tone bar, the last fixed mark in the dimensions
group. With the fixed-surface series exhausted for now, this tick audited the
**documentation truth** half of the package: the figures the catalogue, the
design system, the README and the public JSDoc state in the present tense.

## What was checked

- **The ten checks over all 24 shipped sets** — `themeFailures` returns `[]`.
- **Every numeric claim in `docs/tokens.md` and `docs/design-system.md`** — the
  tables are derived by `docs.test.ts`; the prose figures were checked one at a
  time against the implementation.
- **The panel-action rationale** — repeated in five surfaces (three catalogue
  sections, the design system, the README, `softActionPairs` JSDoc and
  `softAction` JSDoc). `soft-action.test.ts` computed the count only in its
  header; no test read any of the prose.
- **A recomputation of the quoted figures** over `shippedThemes`:

  | Figure | Quoted | Derived |
  | ------ | ------ | ------- |
  | Failing neutral fill-on-tint pairs | 80 (three surfaces), 84 (four) | **84 of 288** |
  | Light luminance band | `0.742–0.765` | `0.657–0.765` |
  | Dark luminance band | `0.012–0.039` | `0.012–0.039` |
  | Worst pair | Windows light `active` on `destructiveSoft`, 1.0042 | **1.0042** |
  | Identity light press | "1.0492" | tightest is `active` on `infoSoft`, **1.0202**; 1.0492 is `active` on `destructiveSoft` |

## Finding

**The panel action's rationale had four owners and three were stale.** The count
moved from 80 to 84 when plans 503 and 619 moved palette values; the documents
did not. The light band was measured before the AAA light tints were considered:
AAA light `destructiveSoft` is `0.657`, outside the quoted `0.742–0.765` range.
The identity figure was true for one pair but presented as "the identity light
press", while the identity's tightest press is `active` on `infoSoft` at
1.0202:1.

No colour is wrong: every shipped set still passes the contract. The failure is
that a palette edit can leave a page quoting a count and a band the package no
longer produces while every pair passes — the same class as plans 336, 536 and
544, on the one paragraph those plans never read.

## Decision

Derive the count, total, both bands and both named ratios in
`soft-action.test.ts` as the shipped guarantee, and read the five quoting
surfaces in `docs.test.ts`: every `N of the M shipped` and every
`N are under the 1.05:1 floor` must equal the derived pair, each surface must
still quote one, and the band and two ratios are matched as exact sentences.
Correct the three 80s, the light band in both documents, and make the identity
sentence name its pair. Plan 637 carries the exact edits.

## Not changed

- Any colour, pair, floor or metric.
- The `softAction` family or its four roles.
- Plan files and `TODO.md` done rows: they record what plan 394 measured at the
  time (80), not a present-tense claim.
