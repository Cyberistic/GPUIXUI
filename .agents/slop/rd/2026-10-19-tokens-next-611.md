# tokens-theme — plan 611 and the next-unit audit (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **611** gives the package a marker for content whose colour it cannot know.
  The ColorPicker interlock named the failure: a selected-swatch ring painted
  with one role measures **1.000:1** on a swatch of that colour, and no single
  palette role can contrast with every swatch, so a two-tone ring is the only
  design that can promise anything.
- `contrastRing(theme)` returns the theme's two extreme tones — `background`
  next to the content, `foreground` outside it — at `borderWidth.strong`. For
  any content colour the two contrasts multiply to the tone pair's own ratio,
  so the worst case over **every** colour is the geometric mean,
  `sqrt(contrast(outer, inner))`: 3.98:1 on the identity light pair, 4.58:1 at
  AAA, **3.70:1** at the tightest shipped set (`Dark · Linux`), all above the
  3:1 mark floor (4.5:1 at AAA).
- The guarantee was not implied by the existing pairs: a 7:1 ink/canvas pair
  clears every text pair and still guarantees only 2.65:1 for the ring. So
  `themeRingFailures(theme, contrast?)` measures the derived bound and
  `themeFailures` composes it as the tenth kind, `ring`, after `contrast`. A
  probe over the 24 shipped sets already clears it, so no colour moved.
- The executed one-element composition (`contrast-ring.example.ts`) paints the
  inner tone as the border and the outer as a zero-blur spread shadow, which
  consumes the runtime's single shadow slot; the docs state the cost and the
  wrapper alternative.
- The reference, design system, README, inventory and ledger document the
  helper, the proof, the cost and the ColorPicker handoff; the type index and
  the value-coverage guard now include `ContrastRing`, `RingFailure`,
  `contrastRing` and `themeRingFailures`.
