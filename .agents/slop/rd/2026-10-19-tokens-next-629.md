## Finding

**The inbox's maximum height counted the list but not the header.** Plan 627
made `notificationPanelMaxHeight ≥ notificationListMaxHeight + 2 *
borderWidth.thin`. `notification-centre.tsx` paints a header above the list:

- `NotificationCentreHeader` — `padding: spacing.lg` on both sides, a
  `borderWidth.thin` bottom rule, a `label` title and two ghost `Button
  size="sm"` actions, each `controlHeight(theme, "sm")` tall;
- `NotificationCentreList` — `maxHeight: 280`;
- `panelStyle` — `maxHeight: 380`, `overflow: "hidden"`, the panel's own
  `borderWidth.thin`.

So the content box must hold `header + list`, not the list alone. The probe:

```ts
extendTheme(getTheme("light"), {
  dimensions: {
    notificationListMaxHeight: 280,
    notificationPanelMaxHeight: 300,
  },
});
```

returned **`[]`** from `themeMetricFailures` and `themeFailures` while the
header is 53px (`2*12 + 28 + 1`) and the panel needs 335 inside its borders. The
panel's `overflow: hidden` therefore squeezes the header, or the list's
documented 280px viewport is unreachable. A second probe with a lifted `sm`
control (40) and `notificationPanelMaxHeight: 300` also returned `[]` while the
need is 347.

## Plan 629 — the notification inbox holds its header

- The bound becomes
  `notificationListMaxHeight + 2*spacing.lg + controlHeight.sm + 3*borderWidth.thin`,
  all from the theme's own metrics. The density rule already holds
  `controlHeight.sm` at least one `label` line tall plus its borders, so the
  header's action is the taller line and no `Math.max` is needed.
- Identity bound 335 (header 53); the shipped 380 clears it at 1.13×, so no
  colour, pair, floor, metric or shipped theme value moves.
- `notification-header-fit.test.ts` states the bug, the shipped pass, the
  identity figures, the old list-and-borders bound (282), the minimum edges
  (335/334), a lifted `sm` control (347) and a theme's own 4px border (344).
- `surface-border-fit.test.ts`, `notification-panel-metrics.test.ts`,
  `docs.test.ts`, the catalogue, design system, README and ledger carry the
  corrected figures; `surface-border-fit`'s coherent fixture lifts the panel
  above its own 388px bound.
- The cross-team rows are untouched: `components` still reads its own literals
  for the panel until its dimension migration lands.
