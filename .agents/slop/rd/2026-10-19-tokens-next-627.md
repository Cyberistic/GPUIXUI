# tokens-theme — audit after plan 625 (2026-10-19, seventh tick)

## Scope

Every remaining `[ ]` row in `TODO.md` is a cross-team handoff or the
upstream-blocked `registerFont` (052), so this tick audited the fixed-surface
bounds plan 620 and plan 625 had just built — the one contract this workstream
owns end to end.

## What was checked

- **The ten checks over all 24 shipped sets** — `themeFailures` returns `[]`.
- **Every `dimensions` fit rule** against the surface the component actually
  paints: field, chip, keycap, date trigger, menu, Toast card, notification
  inbox, dialog and drawer shells.
- **The consumer paint in `packages/components`** for those surfaces —
  `dialog.tsx:188`, `drawer.tsx:195`, `toast/viewport.tsx:132`,
  `notification-centre.tsx:357` and `menu/surface.tsx:61` each set
  `borderWidth: theme.borderWidth.thin` on the element that also carries the
  fixed width/height.
- **The plan-384 precedent**, which established that a fit rule compares the
  container's content box and subtracts the two borders for a control, a field
  and a keycap.

## Finding

**The fixed surfaces compared their outer metric, not their content box.** Plan
384's rule was applied to the controls that predate it and not to the surfaces
that landed later, including plan 625's dialog and drawer rules. A coherent
theme with `borderWidth.thin: 12`, `controlHeight` 48/52/56, `rowHeight: 56`,
`keycapSize: 40`, `calendarCellSize: 48` and `timelineMarkerSize: 12` returned
**`[]`** from `themeMetricFailures` and `themeFailures` with:

| Member | Value | Bound then | Content box | Chrome painted |
| ------ | ----- | ---------- | ----------- | -------------- |
| `dialogWidthSm` | 88 | 88 | 64 | 72 |
| `drawerWidthSm` | 88 | 88 | 64 | 72 |
| `drawerHeightSm` | 140 | 140 | 116 | 140 |
| `toastWidth` | 80 | 66 | 56 | 66 |

The same held for the menu minimum and the inbox width, and the inbox's maximum
height contained the list only before its own two borders.

## Plan 627 — the fixed surfaces fit their own border

- Seven bounds add `2 * borderWidth.thin`: `overlayMinWidth`, `toastWidth`,
  `notificationPanelWidth`, its maximum height, `dialogWidthSm`,
  `drawerWidthSm` and `drawerHeightSm`. The first step of each ladder carries
  the larger sizes; the shipped identity bounds move 53/66/50/88/88/96/280 →
  **55/68/52/90/90/98/282**, and every shipped set clears them, so no value
  moves.
- `surface-border-fit.test.ts` states the bug in its header and covers the
  shipped pass, the seven identity bounds, each surface failing on its own, a
  theme that reads its own border (4, not the fixture's 12), and the total
  check.
- The three suites that already guarded these rules are updated to the new
  bounds, not deleted: `fixed-surface-fit.test.ts`, `shell-chrome-fit.test.ts`
  and `notification-panel-metrics.test.ts`.
- The catalogue, design system, README, JSDoc and ledger state the content-box
  rule and the figures; `docs.test.ts` derives them.
