## Finding

**The Toast card's dismiss control was never counted.** Plan 620 gave
`toastWidth` its first bound and plan 627 added the card's borders, but the
content row in `toast/viewport.tsx` also paints an unconditional
`IconButton label="Dismiss" size="sm"` after a second `spacing.lg` gap:

- the content row is `flexDirection: "row"`, `gap: theme.spacing.lg`,
  `padding: theme.spacing.lg`;
- the `md` tone icon, the flexible title column, an optional action `Button`
  and the dismiss `IconButton` are its children;
- `iconButtonStyle` in `internal/button.tsx` sets `width: side, height: side`
  with `side = controlHeight(theme, size)`, so the dismiss is a square `sm`
  control;
- the card has `overflow: "hidden"`.

The probe:

```ts
extendTheme(getTheme("light"), { dimensions: { toastWidth: 100 } });
```

returned **`[]`** from `themeMetricFailures` and `themeFailures` while the card
needs `24 + 16 + 12 + 14 + 12 + 28 + 2 = 108`: the dismiss control is painted
outside the card and the toast cannot be closed. A second, coherent probe with
a lifted `sm` control (`controlHeight.sm: 48`, `label.lineHeight: 40`,
`rowHeight: 60`, `calendarCellSize: 48`) and `toastWidth: 90` also returned
`[]` while the need is 128.

At the shipped metrics the old bound is exactly 68 (`67` fails, `68` passes);
the new bound is 108 and the shipped 380 clears it at 3.52×.
