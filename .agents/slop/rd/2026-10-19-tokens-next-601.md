# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 601 (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **601** closed the date trigger's fixed width and a false claim beside it.
  `date-picker.tsx:272` paints a 220px trigger around its `spacing.md` insets, a
  `spacing.md` gap, two borders and a `caption` chevron; the width was a literal,
  so no brand could own it. `dimensions.dateFieldWidth` now owns it, and a
  content-fit rule reports a field narrower than `3 × spacing.md + 2 × thin +
  label.fontSize + caption.fontSize` (51px on the identity light set).
- The same token was documented as "default popover, combobox and calendar
  width". The calendar is content-sized at seven `calendarCellSize` cells
  (238px at 34px per cell, before the popover's padding), and the popover has
  its own 200px minimum (already a cross-team row). `overlayWidth` now names the
  combobox and navigation menu, and both documents derive the calendar grid from
  `calendarCellSize`.
- 155 files / 5,982 tests green; `check`, `lint` and `test` pass. No colour,
  pair, floor or other shipped metric moves.
- Both directions verified: removing the `atLeast` block fails the 40px and
  boundary cases; removing the docs sentence fails the new `docs.test.ts` case.

## Candidates, re-checked after 601

1. **The fixed overlay consumers.** `popover.tsx:406` paints a 200px minimum
   where the token set has `overlayMinWidth` 180, and `combobox.tsx:677` scrolls
   at 280 where `overlayMaxHeight` is 320. Both are already one cross-team row
   (plan 353 audit); the token side is correct and needs no new member unless
   the component owner decides a compact overlay is a real concept.
2. **The 16/18 field-adornment split.** `search-input.tsx:175` and
   `time-picker.tsx:444` paint an 18×18 clear control around a 14px glyph, while
   `fieldAdornmentSize` is 16 and three other components use 16. The token is a
   slot, not a glyph tier; a lower bound against `iconSize.sm` would over-
   constrain a brand that wants a smaller adornment. The consumer migration is
   the existing cross-team row.
3. **The date picker's `CELL`.** Still a 34px literal, already the
   `components` handoff from plan 556 (`calendarCellSize`); plan 601 adds the
   trigger width to that tree's row.
4. **The timeline lane and toast bars.** 16/8px lane/dot and 3/2px bars are
   decorations, not surfaces a theme clips content inside; the plan-598 audit
   already argued why they stay out.
5. **The keycap at 20px.** `keyboard-shortcuts.tsx:169` fixes a 20px square, but
   the keycap grows for multi-key shortcuts in practice; the token is already
   the minimum and the consumer migration is cross-team.

## Order

The token-native surface is closed again for the fixed-field family. The next
unit should come from a consumer composition that paints a role with no pair, or
a property the package claims without measuring — not from another decoration.
A new dimension must be a surface a component clips or places content inside.
