# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 540 (2026-10-18)

Where the workstream stands after plan 540.

## What landed

- **540** closed the last domain the total check could not see: the **alpha
  channel**. `themeColorFailures` accepts six- and eight-digit hex, so
  `shadow: "#191D2580"` passed every check while `elevationShadow` wrote the
  level's own 12% into the fourth byte (the authored 50% gone), and
  `overlay: "#000000"` passed while a solid scrim hid the window the role exists
  to veil. `themeOpacityFailures` is the seventh check: `translucentRoles` is
  the index of the three veils (`selection`, `overlay`, `selectionOnFill`), each
  needs a byte strictly between `00` and `FF`, every other role is opaque, and
  `roleForm` is the total rule. A non-hex value stays the colour check's
  diagnostic. Two stale claims in the same area were corrected: the "third
  check" ordinal in `docs/tokens.md` and the README's claim that the composition
  suites run three separate checks. No colour, pair, floor or metric moved.

## Candidates, re-checked

1. **A scrim-direction rule (rejected).** A light theme could ship a *light*
   scrim that lightens instead of veiling, and no check reports it; but the AAA
   dark set legitimately veils with light because a black veil cannot darken
   pure black, so a "veil toward the canvas in the mode's direction" rule would
   be false for a shipped theme. The surface and dialog-face pairs are the
   measurable part.
2. **A multi-entry elevation level (rejected here).** `elevationShadows`
   resolves every entry for a compositing renderer, while GPUix paints one
   shadow and the contrast walk reads `elevationShadow` (the first). A second
   entry is finite- and alpha-checked by the metric contract and paints nothing
   in the pinned runtime, so there is no composition to measure. Revisit if a
   compositing renderer path ships.
3. **The pointer-state inverse veil (still the documented gap).** Plan 513's
   60-of-360 figure stands: no single neutral alpha clears the on-colour floor
   and the 1.05:1 veil floor on the AAA light set. `userSelect: "none"` on a
   control's chrome is the recommendation; not a pair.
4. **The consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.
