# tokens-theme — audit after plan 634 (2026-10-19, twelfth tick)

## Scope

Plan 634's audit parked the Toast's 3px tone bar as the next candidate: it is a
fixed width the card paints inside itself, but it had no dimension token and
plan 631 excluded it from the card's content bound as "consumer chrome".

## What was checked

- **The ten checks over all 24 shipped sets** — `themeFailures` returns `[]`.
- **`ToastCard` as painted** in
  `workspaces/components/packages/components/src/toast/viewport.tsx`: the outer
  card is a row with its two borders and `overflow: "hidden"`; the first child
  is `width: 3, alignSelf: "stretch"`; the second is the `flexGrow: 1` content
  row with `spacing.lg` padding and gap, the `md` tone icon, the flexible title
  column, the optional action and the always-painted square `sm` dismiss.
- **The dimensions group** — every other fixed mark is a token: the unread dot
  6, carousel 8/18, timeline 8/16, keycap 20, presence dot 10. The bar was the
  last painted mark with no owner.
- **The existing bound** — `2 * spacing.lg + iconSize.md + spacing.lg +
  bodyStrong.fontSize + spacing.lg + controlHeight.sm + 2 * borderWidth.thin =
  108`; the content row at that width is `108 - 2 - 3 = 103` while its chrome
  needs `106`.

## Finding

**The Toast card's tone bar was neither owned nor charged.** A copied theme at
110 passed every check while the title glyph needed 111:

```ts
const clipped = extendTheme(getTheme("light"), {
  dimensions: { toastWidth: 110 },
});
themeMetricFailures(clipped); // [] before the rule
```

and `dimensions.toastToneBarWidth` did not exist, so a brand could not edit the
bar at all — against plan 353's obligation to own the fixed surface metrics
components still hardcode.

## Plan 636 — the Toast's tone bar is a token the card holds

- `toastToneBarWidth` (3) ships in `dimensions` beside `toastWidth`.
- The card bound charges it: identity **108 → 111**; shipped `380` clears at
  `3.42×` (was `3.52×`).
- `toast-chrome-fit.test.ts` owns the member, plants `toastWidth: 111` under a
  6px bar (bound 114), keeps the shaken-out 111/131/159 figures and states the
  shipped headroom; `fixed-surface-fit.test.ts` and `surface-border-fit.test.ts`
  follow the corrected bound.
- The catalogue gains the row and the expression; the `toastWidth` row, the
  design system, the inventory and the JSDoc name the bar.
- No colour, pair, floor or other metric moves; `toastWidth` stays 380.
- The components migration joins the existing `theme.dimensions` cross-team
  row; no component file is edited here.

## Verification
