# tokens-theme — audit before plan 651 (2026-10-19, twenty-second tick)

## Scope

Every remaining `[ ]` row in `TODO.md` is a cross-team handoff or the
upstream-blocked `registerFont` (052). This tick audited the third state of a
selectable row: what a **selected** row paints while it is pressed.

## What was probed

Plans `381` and `619` completed the row's hover → selection step and the press of
an *unselected* row (`accent` → `active`). `docs/design-system.md` §8 states that
a chosen row "still presses to `active` … and walk[s] away from the canvas like
every ladder (`stateLadders`)". The probe printed `contrastRatio` and
`relativeLuminance` for `selected` and `active` in every shipped default
palette:

| Set | `selected` | `active` | step | direction |
| --- | ---------- | -------- | ---- | --------- |
| Light | `#E0E3FF` | `#E1E5EC` | **1.001:1** | toward |
| High contrast light | `#D8DBFF` | `#D6D6D6` | 1.072:1 | away |
| Dark | `#402F94` | `#2E3543` | 1.203:1 | toward |
| High contrast dark | `#33257A` | `#3D3D3D` | 1.146:1 | away |
| macOS light | `#CFE3FA` | `#DCDCE2` | 1.042:1 | away |
| macOS dark | `#1A4568` | `#404042` | 1.032:1 | away |
| Linux light | `#D7E6F9` | `#DEDDDB` | 1.071:1 | away |
| Linux dark | `#27476A` | `#464646` | 1.013:1 | toward |
| Windows light | `#C2E2FF` | `#E0E0E0` | 1.019:1 | toward |
| Windows dark | `#094771` | `#454545` | 1.018:1 | away |
| Windows XP light | `#BDD4FF` | `#D8D2B7` | 1.015:1 | away |
| Windows 7 light | `#CCE4FA` | `#D5E3F4` | **1.005:1** | toward |

Eleven of twelve are under the 1.05:1 step floor, the worst is identity light at
1.001:1, and eight move in the wrong direction. `themeContrastFailures` and
`themeDirectionFailures` both returned `[]`: neither list names the composition.

## Finding

The selected row's press is a different state from a filled control's press.
Pressing a chosen row previews the row it will return to if the click
deselects it, so the fill moves **toward** the canvas — the one ladder that runs
the other way. `stateLadders` assumed every ladder steps away from the canvas,
so a toward ladder could not exist, and no role carried the fill.

## Options considered

1. **Move `active`.** Rejected: `active` is also one step away from `hover`
   (`washStepPairs`) and one step from the unselected row's `accent` hover
   (`rowPressStepPairs`). Identity light would need `active` ~0.04 luminance
   lighter, which inverts the wash ladder.
2. **Deepen `selected` until `active` is a step away.** This works — the probe
   cleared the step — but forces `placeholder`, `track`, `border` and
   `skeleton` to move in six light palettes and four dark ones (about forty
   values) to keep the pairs they already pass, because those roles sit just
   off `selected`. The library would rebalance its neutrals for one pointer
   state.
3. **A canvas veil over `selected`** — chosen. `selectedActive` is
   `compositeOver("#FFFFFF3D", selected)` in light and
   `compositeOver("#0000003D", selected)` in dark: 24% of the canvas colour,
   shipped as an editable opaque literal. It moves toward the canvas, improves
   every text floor (dark text on a lighter light fill, light text on a darker
   dark fill), and leaves the rest/track/border/skeleton relationships intact.
   The probe ran all ten checks on all 24 shipped sets with the derived values
   and **no existing value moved**; the tightest step is Linux light at
   **1.061:1**.

## Plan

Plan 651 ships the role, the step pair, the ink/chrome pairs and a per-ladder
`direction` on `StateLadder`; the transient state stays outside the
selection-wash, scrim and elevation walks, like the other pointer washes
(plan 359). The `components` migration — paint `selectedActive` on a selected
row's press, and keep `selected` on hover — is a cross-team row.

## Not changed

- any existing colour value, floor, figure or metric;
- the text-selection, scrim and elevation lists (transient pointer state);
- `selected` under hover (a selected row does not return to `accent`);
- a component or primitive source file;
- the check-kind count.
