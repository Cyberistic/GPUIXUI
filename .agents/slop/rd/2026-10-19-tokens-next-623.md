## Finding

**The selected row's label had no relationship to its title.** A selected row
paints its title in `foreground` and its inherited label in
`selectedForeground`. Both roles have their own contrast pairs, but `textTiers`
named no pair for the relationship, so a theme could raise the label to the
title's strength — or above it — while every check returned `[]`.

The planted identity dark theme:

| Role | Value | What it paints |
| ---- | ----- | -------------- |
| `selectedForeground` | `#FFFFFF` | the inherited label on a selected row |
| `foreground` | unchanged | the row's title, `ListItemTitle`'s colour |

The label measures **10.23:1** on `selected` against the title's **9.03:1** —
the secondary tier is brighter than the body tier — and
`themeContrastFailures(theme)`, `themeTextTierFailures(theme)` and
`themeFailures(theme)` all returned **`[]`**.

The three documents argued the pair could not exist: “Adding it would demand a
step the design does not have and report every shipped set.” That is false —
every shipped set already clears a 1.05:1 step, and the tightest is **1.106:1**
(`Dark · Windows` on `hover`).

## Plan 623 — the selected row's label below its title

- `textTiers` gains `["selectedForeground", "foreground"]` after the two state
  pairs. `themeTextTierFailures` already compares two roles on the surfaces both
  are documented on, so the shared-surface derivation limits the pair to
  `hover`, `active`, `accent` and `selected` — the four fills a selected row
  keeps — with no change to the function.
- `selected-label-tier.test.ts` states the defect in its header and covers the
  shipped pass, the 1.106:1 tightest figure, a label raised to title strength, a
  label brighter than the title, and the state-pair surface list.
- `figures.ts` gains the tier figure so `docs/tokens.md` derives the 1.106:1
  sentence; the figure table grows 28 → 29. `figures.test.ts` excludes the new
  state pair from the four body-role tiers and quotes the new relationship in the
  design-system sentence.
- The three misleading “deliberately not a body tier … would report every
  shipped set” paragraphs are replaced with what the pair is: a state tier on
  the four row fills.
