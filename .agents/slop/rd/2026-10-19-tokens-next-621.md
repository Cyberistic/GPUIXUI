## Finding

**The time segment was held to half the digits it paints.** `TimePickerSegment`
renders an `<input>` with the padded two-digit `HH`/`MM` value at `padding: 0`
and `textAlign: "center"`, but `timeSegmentWidth` was checked against **one
`body` em**. A copied theme could return `themeFailures(theme) === []` while the
segment showed barely one digit:

| Body | Segment | Old rule | What the segment paints |
| ---- | ------- | -------- | ----------------------- |
| 14 (shipped) | 26 | passes | `HH` fits |
| 24 | 24 | **passes (24 ≥ 24)** | two digits need ~29px |
| 22 | 26 | **passes (26 ≥ 22)** | two digits need ~26.4px |

No shipped component reads the token yet (the migration is already recorded),
so no shipped composition breaks today; the defect is that the guarantee a
brand is told to rely on is incomplete. It is the same class plan 384 fixed for
a chip and plan 620 for a toast, inbox and menu row.

## Plan 621 — the time segment holds two digits

- The bound is two tabular digits at a 0.65em advance, rounded up:
  `Math.ceil(2 * 0.65 * body.fontSize)`. At the shipped 14px body it is 19, so
  the shipped 26px segment still passes (1.37×) and **no token value moves**.
- The rounding keeps the bound a paintable pixel; without it, a body-24 bound
  of `31.200000000000003` would reject a theme that wrote `31.2`.
- The rule names its font assumption: a theme that names a face with wider
  digits must widen the segment. Two ems were rejected as an over-constraint;
  `lineHeight` was rejected because a theme may ship `lineHeight === fontSize`.
- `time-segment-fit.test.ts` states the bug in its header and covers the shipped
  pass and headroom, the shipped bound, a narrow segment at the shipped body, a
  raised body that moves the bound, the exact-boundary pass and the total check.
  It fails **3/6** on the pre-fix tree and passes **6/6** after.
- The old one-em case in `fixed-surface-metrics.test.ts` moves to the new bound;
  the catalogue, design system, ledger and `scale.ts` JSDoc state the rule.
