## Candidates, re-checked

1. ~~The positive dimensions and border widths are hand-named~~ — closed by 554.
2. **Fills on row washes.** `secondary` on `hover` measures 1.000:1 in the
   identity sets, so a neutral filled control inside a hovered row has no shape.
   The shipped consumer ladder is the plan-357/334 cross-team row; the token
   contract already reports the fill on the four panels (`stateFillSurfacePairs`)
   but a control is not a panel. A token-native unit would need a decision about
   which surface a *nested* control's fill is measured against; defer until a
   consumer paints it.
3. **The theme-choice URL boundary.** Exhaustive axis combinations, canonical
   re-encoding, stale-axis clearing and a real `URLSearchParams` round-trip are
   all guarded (`theme-choice-params.test.ts`). No gap.
4. **Extra colour keys.** `extendTheme` keeps an unknown colour key, but
   `themeColorFailures`/`themeOpacityFailures` walk `colorRoles`; an extra key is
   not read by any shipped component. That is the same reasoning as raw palette
   ramps — not a contract hole.
5. **Consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.
