# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 595 (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **595** closed the last *surface* the interactive-fill contract omitted. A
  field hosts its own controls: `NumberField` paints its chrome with the `input`
  fill (`fieldRecipeStyle`) and its two step buttons with the ghost-control
  resolver, so their `hover`/`active` washes land on the field's own fill.
  `stateFillSurfaces` named only the canvas, card, popover and quiet panel, so an
  edited theme with `input = hover` returned `[]` from all nine checks while the
  stepper's wash measured **1.000:1** (the same wash on the card was still
  1.133:1). The field fill joins the list: 60 → 75 fill-on-surface pairs and
  405 → 420 in the contract. A probe over all 24 shipped sets found every fill
  already above 1.05:1 on `input` — tightest **1.1176:1**, `accent` on `input`
  in `Dark · Windows` — so no colour moved.
- 152 files / 5,966 tests green; `check`, `lint` and `test` pass. Both
  directions verified: removing `input` fails the regression and pair cases; the
  document cases failed before the three pages were updated.

## Candidates, re-checked after 595

1. **Other text roles on the neutral pointer states.** A full role × surface
   probe still flags `mutedForeground` / `placeholder` / `disabledForeground` on
   `secondaryHover`/`secondaryActive` (4.29–4.33:1 in the AAA sets) and on
   `softActionHover`/`softActionActive`. Each would be a pair without a shipped
   composition: the secondary Button paints `secondaryForeground`, a menu row
   hovers `hover`/`active` rather than the designed neutral family, a disabled
   control does not step, and a field or hint is not painted inside a raised
   panel action. Left out — a guard with no failing plant is not a unit.
2. **`placeholder` on `successSoft` (4.495:1).** A field inside a tone-tinted
   Alert is not a documented composition (the Alert carries an action, a
   checkbox or a caption, not a field), and a one-hundredth miss in one AAA
   palette would need a colour move for a paint nothing ships. Left out.
3. **The find-highlight body floor.** `themeHighlightFailures` holds every
   matchable role to the secondary floor by design: no perceivable wash keeps a
   syntax token at body strength. Still documented, not a gap.
4. **`selectedForeground` as a `textTiers` member.** A selected row's title
   (`foreground`) and inherited label (`selectedForeground`) sit side by side,
   but plan 500 deliberately records `selectedForeground` as a
   secondary-strength tint rather than a body tier. Revisit only with a shipped
   composition that relies on the distinction.
5. **Remaining consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.
