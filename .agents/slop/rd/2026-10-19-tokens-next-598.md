## Candidates, re-checked after 598

1. **The last unowned marks — the timeline lane and the toast bars.** Timeline
   paints a 16px lane around an 8px dot (`timeline.tsx:151-157`); the Toast
   paints a 3px tone bar and a 2px progress bar (`toast/viewport.tsx:141`,
   `:229-240`). These are marks and chrome thicknesses, not surfaces: a theme
   has no clipping relationship to break, and the 4px carousel precedent was a
   *replacement* mark (idle → active), not a decoration. Left out until a
   consumer composition makes one a shape a theme must own; do not add guards
   with no failing plant.
2. **The date field's 220px trigger width.** `date-picker.tsx:296` fixes the
   trigger at 220px while `dimensions.overlayWidth` is documented as the
   "default popover, combobox and calendar width". The calendar itself is
   content-sized (seven `calendarCellSize` cells ≈ 250px including gaps), so the
   JSDoc's "calendar width" is likely wrong and the trigger is a *field* width,
   not an overlay width. A future unit should either name a
   `dateFieldWidth`-style metric (if a fixed date field is a design concept) or
   correct the overlay claim and point the consumer at the right token. Not
   shipped now: the component migration is cross-team and the claim needs a
   probe against the rendered popover first.
3. **The unread dot's colour.** The 6px marker paints `primary` on the row's
   `accent`/`hover`/`active` fill; macOS dark measures 2.836:1 on `active` and
   2.748:1 on `selected`, under the mark floor. `primaryText` clears both. This
   is already a `components` handoff (plan 585 audit) and `primaryText` already
   has the pairs; no token work.
4. **`selectedForeground` as a body tier.** Still deliberately not one (plan
   500/525); no shipped composition relies on the distinction.
5. **The consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.

## Order

The token-native surface is closed again for panel metrics. The next unit should
come from a property the package *claims* without measuring — the
`overlayWidth`/calendar sentence above is the current strongest candidate —
or a consumer composition that paints a role the contract does not measure, with
a probe that fails first. A new dimension must be a surface a component clips or
places content inside; a decoration is not a token.
