# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 556 (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **556** closed a promise the design system already made: §6 said a brand could
  widen a **dialog** through `theme.dimensions`, while `dialog.tsx` kept
  `MAX_WIDTH` as a local `{ sm: 360, md: 480, lg: 640 }`. The same audit found
  the calendar day cell (34), the time segment (26) and the avatar presence dot
  (10) as the same class of consumer literal.

  `dimensions` gains six members (`dialogWidthSm/Md/Lg`, `calendarCellSize`,
  `timeSegmentWidth`, `avatarPresenceDotSize`), every shipped theme, platform
  variant and recipe inherits them, and `themeMetricFailures` holds a dialog
  ladder to grow, a day cell to fit the small control, a segment to be at least
  one body em and the dot to stay at most half the smallest avatar. A
  `usableBound` guard skips a fit rule whose bound is already the positive
  rule's diagnostic, so the existing zero-avatar probe keeps one report. The
  docs, README and ledger name the members; the four consumer migrations are
  cross-team rows. 138 files / 5,694 tests; `check`, `lint`, `test` green. No
  colour, pair, floor, type or existing metric moves.

## Candidates, re-checked

1. **The empty-state prose measure (`empty-state.tsx`, 360px).** A text measure,
   not a surface or a mark; the theme carries no font-metric model to bound it,
   so tokenising it would ship a number with no relationship. Revisit only if
   the system adds measure tokens.
2. **The toast tone bar (`toast/viewport.tsx`, 3px).** A decorative accent
   beside the tone glyph; the glyph carries the status. The carousel and avatar
   marks encode state, which is why they are dimensions. Keep as a local design
   detail until a consumer needs it.
3. **The popover's 200px minimum and the combobox's 280px scroll height.** Both
   already have `components` rows (plan 353 audit): read `overlayMinWidth` /
   `overlayMaxHeight` or settle a compact overlay concept. Not a token gap until
   that decision lands.
4. **The timeline dot (8px).** Exactly `spacing.sm`; the consumer can read the
   spacing scale. No new dimension.
5. **Consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.
