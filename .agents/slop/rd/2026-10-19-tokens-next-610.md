# tokens-theme — plan 610 and the next-unit audit (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **610** closes the last exclusion the modal-scrim and floating-shadow
  contracts carried. `disabled`, `skeleton` and `track` were left out of
  `contentFillSurfaces` with the written rationale that they "are not the content
  a dialog opens over". That is false for the exclusion it justified: the scrim's
  job is to make the window recede, and a disabled control, a loading row and a
  value groove are resting fills — not pointer states — that stay on screen while
  the dialog is open.
- A probe over the 24 shipped sets already clears the three compositions
  (1.284:1 scrim at the tightest, Dark · Windows on `track`; 1.155:1 dialog face
  over the new fills; 1.245:1 `md` shadow), so no colour moved.
- The hole was a copy-and-own hole: a dark theme with the three roles set to
  `#000000` passes all nine checks while the shipped black scrim composites to
  **1.000:1** on each.
- The three fills join `contentFillSurfaces`, so they are measured by
  `overlayWashPairs`, `dialogSurfacePairs` and the `md` elevation walk together.
  The scrim and dialog-face walks grow 18 → **21**; the elevation content list 12
  → **15**; `contrastPairs` stays **420** because those walks are published
  separately (the same shape as the elevation walk).
- The rationale is corrected in `contrast.ts`, `docs/tokens.md`,
  `docs/design-system.md` (§7, §9 and the floor-table rows) and the package
  README; `COMPONENTS.md` states the new walk counts; `TODO.md` records the row.
