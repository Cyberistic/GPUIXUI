# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 546 (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **547** closed the gap between the catalogue's "one place" promise and the
  fill list behind it. `docs/tokens.md` said "`stateFillRoles` is the fill list
  and `stateFillSurfaces` the surface list, so a fifth surface or a new filled
  family joins one place", but `stateFillRoles` was a hand-typed 15-entry array
  that retyped the four families from `filledControlFamilies`. Every other
  family consumer already derives from the index — `filledControl`,
  `stateStepPairs`, `stateLadders` (the direction contract) and
  `selectionFillRoles` — so a fifth family added where the docs say to add it
  gained its steps, direction, selection pairs and helper, but no
  fill-on-surface pair. A pressed control whose fill matched its panel would
  have returned `[]` from `themeContrastFailures`.

  A probe on the pre-fix tree added the near-complete `warning` family (only
  `warningHover`/`warningActive` were missing) to the index, made `warningHover`
  equal the canvas through `extendTheme`, and the checker returned no
  `warningHover`-on-`background` pair; with the derivation in place the same
  probe reports the pair. The list now derives from the family index and the
  suite's expectation does too, so the old hand list is gone from both the
  contract and its guard.

  131 files / 5641 tests green (one case replaced, not added); `check`, `lint`
  and `test` pass. No colour role, pair, floor, metric, theme, platform style,
  recipe or document value moves.
