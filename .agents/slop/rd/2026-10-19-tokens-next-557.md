# tokens-theme — next-unit audit after plan 557 (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **557** closed the last composition the caret story missed. Plan 511 gave
  `nativeFieldTheme` the `primary` **fill** because the accent is a `mark` pair
  on the `input` fill and the four `Surface` tones. Plan 527 then established
  that `InlineEdit` swaps a row's text for a field in place, on the four row
  washes, and the caret inherits there. On macOS dark the fill measures
  **2.748:1** on `selected` and **2.836:1** on `active`, under the 3:1 mark
  floor, so a caret in a selected inline edit vanished while every check
  returned `[]`.

  The caret is now the accent's readable **ink** (`primaryText`), and
  `caretPairs` publishes the one surface the text contract did not already hold
  — the field's own `input` fill. The other eight are `primaryTextPairs` at the
  stricter `secondary` floor. `contrastPairs` grows 404 → 405; every shipped set
  clears the new pair (7.831:1 at the tightest, Linux dark) and no colour moves.
  The `nativeFieldTheme` JSDoc, `docs/tokens.md`, `docs/design-system.md`, the
  README, `COMPONENTS.md` and the plan index state the role and the recorded
  failure. 138 files / 5,706 tests; `check`, `lint`, `test` green. Both
  directions were run: with the helper reverted to `primary` five cases fail;
  with `...caretPairs` removed from the contract two cases (and three
  composition suites) fail.

## Candidates, re-checked

1. **The empty-state prose measure (`empty-state.tsx`, 360px).** A text measure,
   not a surface or a mark; the theme carries no font-metric model to bound it.
2. **The toast tone bar (`toast/viewport.tsx`, 3px).** A decorative accent
   beside the tone glyph; the glyph carries the status.
3. **The popover's 200px minimum and the combobox's 280px scroll height.** Both
   already have `components` rows (plan 353 audit); not a token gap until that
   decision lands.
4. **The timeline dot (8px).** Exactly `spacing.sm`; no new dimension.
5. **A `caret` colour role.** Rejected in plan 557 §6: `primaryText` already
   clears the floor on every surface a field sits on, and a 71st role for a 1px
   line would force every platform style and recipe to name it.
6. **Consumer handoffs.** Another workstream's tree.

## Order

Nothing token-native is queued. The next unit should come from an audit of a
composition a consumer actually paints, or from a new mission requirement. The
caret audit is a useful template: a role borrowed from one tonality for a
composition the package documents in another — fills as marks, fills as text,
washes as marks — is where the remaining blind spots hide.
