## Finding

**The ledger's check counts had no owner.** Plan 611 made `ring` the tenth check
`themeFailures` composes, but the **Total theme check** row still said
`inventory.test.ts` held "this row's nine names"; its present-tense "the ten
kinds" was correct and unguarded; and four historical audits in the contrast and
metric rows still retyped `six`/`nine` checks, against plan 567's rule that
history keeps its facts, not its totals. The guard iterated `themeChecks` for
names and order and never read a numeral, so an eleventh kind would leave the
row quoting the old size while every suite stayed green.

No token value, colour pair, floor, metric, platform style or shipped theme
moved, so no example or docs page was added (the precedent is plan 616).

## Plan 617 — the ledger's counts

- The name sentence is count-free: "this row's names and order".
- The one present-tense count is held to `countWord(themeChecks.length)`; the
  four historical audits say "every check".
- `inventory.test.ts` gains one case that builds the expected kind word from the
  index, requires the count-free name sentence, and bans a word-numeral
  `<number> checks` phrase in the three rows. Three synthetic assertions prove
  the pattern rejects `six`/`nine` and accepts "every check".
- Both directions observed: with `COMPONENTS.md` stashed the new case fails on
  "nine names"; restored, it passes. The other direction is the four historical
  phrases, which the pattern matches before the edit and does not after.
