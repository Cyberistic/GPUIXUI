# tokens-theme — plan 609 and the next-unit audit (2026-10-19)

## What landed

- **609** closes the focus indicator a filled control paints **inside** its own
  box. `focusRing`'s `ring` role is designed for the surface *behind* a control
  and the contract measures it there; three shipped consumers paint an inset
  border instead — `primitives`' clipped ButtonGroup, `components`' DatePicker
  "today" ring and `primitives`' pressed selection controls — and on the fill
  `ring` is **1.000:1** on `primary` in **16 of the 24 shipped sets** (both
  identity dark themes, every platform light half, the dark half of Linux,
  Windows, XP and Aero, and every AAA dark half); the best any set reaches is
  1.374:1.
- `insetFocusRing(theme, family)` returns the family's on-colour at
  `borderWidth.strong`. The on-colour pairs already hold that ink on the
  resting, hover and pressed fill, so the helper needs no new role: the tightest
  shipped composition is **4.697:1** (Light · macOS), above both the 3:1 default
  and the 4.5:1 AAA boundary. It is a **border**, not a shadow: `BoxShadow` has
  no inset flag in 0.10.0.
- The suite derives the helper from all 24 sets × 4 families × 3 fills, pins the
  16-name `ring` failure list, proves a copied theme owns both fields, runs the
  executed example, and holds the catalogue, design system and README to the
  sentence. The example is a real clipped button at `sm`/`md`/`lg`.
- No colour, pair, floor, pair count, metric or shipped theme moves. The three
  cross-team rows now name the helper and keep their diagnoses; the ButtonGroup
  row's stale "seven sets" figure (which also listed AAA light as equal) is
  corrected to the measured 16.

## Candidates, re-checked after 609

1. A fifth filled family, a fifth status tone or a new platform joins the
   index-based contracts automatically (`filledControlFamilies`, `statusTones`,
   `platformNames`) except `knownPlatform` in `platforms.ts`, which still retypes
   `"darwin" | "linux" | "win32"`. That is a **maintenance hazard, not a
   behaviour defect** today: a new platform name would resolve to `default` on
   its own OS. It is not worth a plan until a platform is actually added; the
   workstream's own audit rule says the next unit should come from a measured
   consumer failure, and no shipped composition fails.
2. The remaining focus work is consumer-side: the three rows above use the
   helper, and the Toast/NumberField/selection-control states keep their own
   cross-team rows.
3. The native theme gallery still does not paint an inset focus ring; that is a
   playground candidate, not a token change.

## Order

The token contract, the numeric contract, the native bridge and the documents
are closed for the shipped set. The next unit should come from a consumer
composition with a measured failure, not another decorative metric.
