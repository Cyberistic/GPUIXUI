title: Date picker
description: One day, chosen from a month grid in a popover.
---

`DatePicker` is a field-shaped trigger plus a month grid in a **Popover**. GPUix
has no calendar primitive and no native date input, so the grid is ours: pure
`DateTime` arithmetic in `date-picker/calendar.ts`, no `Date`, no `Intl`.

```tsx
import {
  DatePicker,
  DatePickerCalendar,
  DatePickerContent,
  DatePickerTrigger,
} from "@gpuixui/components/date-picker";

const [due, setDue] = useState<string | null>("2026-10-30");

<DatePicker value={due} onValueChange={setDue}>
  <DatePickerTrigger label="Due date" />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

## Value format

The public value is an **ISO day** — `YYYY-MM-DD` — or `null` for "no date".
Never a `DateTime` and never a locale-formatted string: a stored value must not
depend on how it is displayed. `undefined` means uncontrolled.

```tsx
<DatePicker defaultValue="2026-10-30">
  <DatePickerTrigger />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

## Open state

The popover is uncontrolled by default. `open` makes it controlled and
`onOpenChange` reports every request; `defaultOpen` is the uncontrolled initial
state. The calendar still reports the chosen ISO day through `onValueChange`.

```tsx
const [open, setOpen] = useState(false);

<DatePicker
  open={open}
  onOpenChange={setOpen}
  value={due}
  onValueChange={setDue}
>
  <DatePickerTrigger label="Due date" />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

The controlled state is authoritative for the painted surface: a close request
the caller ignores leaves the calendar open. Focus follows the accepted close —
the trigger is focused when `open` actually becomes `false` — so a refused close
never pulls focus out of a surface that is still visible. The trigger's
`aria-expanded` follows the resolved state in both modes.

## Anatomy

| Part | Responsibility |
| ---- | -------------- |
| `DatePicker` | the value, the bounds, the open state and the trigger registry |
| `DatePickerTrigger` | the field-shaped control: the selected day or `placeholder`, `aria-expanded`, a chevron |
| `DatePickerContent` | the package's Popover surface: anchored layer, dismiss stack, Escape focus-return |
| `DatePickerCalendar` | the month header, weekday row, day grid, key handling and the Clear row |

The root owns the value and the bounds because selection and clamping need them;
the calendar owns only the view month and the focused day, which reset when the
surface opens. The grid is always six rows, so the popover does not change height
between a 28-day February and a 31-day month. The leading and trailing cells are
the **real days of the adjacent months**, painted in muted text; choosing one
selects that day and closes, so the grid has no dead cells.

Browsing months changes the **view**, not the value. Only Enter, Space or a click
selects.

## Choosing a month

The header's month label is a control: pressing it opens a **12-month chooser**
for the viewed year. In the chooser the header's chevrons step **years**, the
label becomes the year and returns to the day grid. Choosing a month moves the
view to it and keeps the focused day-of-month (31 January → 28 February in a
common year), then returns to the grid. **The chooser never writes the value**:
only Enter, Space or a click on a day does.

- The viewed month is marked; the chooser is a roving grid of one tab stop.
- A month with no day inside `min`/`max` is muted, leaves the tab order, is named
  `, unavailable`, and does not navigate.
- A year whose every month is outside the bounds disables its year chevron, so a
  press never enters an empty year.
- The chooser shows **one year at a time**; there is no decade list. Step years
  with its chevrons; `PageUp`/`PageDown` on a day still step one month.

## Bounds

`min` and `max` are **inclusive** ISO days. A bound that is absent or malformed
is ignored, exactly like a malformed `value`.

```tsx
<DatePicker
  min="2026-10-17"
  max="2026-12-31"
  value={checkIn}
  onValueChange={setCheckIn}
>
  <DatePickerTrigger label="Check-in" />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

- Days outside the bounds (including an adjacent-month day) are muted, leave the
  tab order and do not activate.
- Arrow, Home/End and PageUp/PageDown movement **clamps** to the bounds instead
  of stepping onto an unavailable day.
- The previous/next month controls disable at the bound's month, so a visible
  month always contains a selectable day.
- In the month chooser a month outside the bounds is inert and named as
  unavailable, and a year chevron whose entire year is outside the bounds is
  disabled.
- Bounds constrain **choosing**, not a controlled `value`: a value outside the
  bounds is displayed as the consumer supplied it.
- `min` after `max` makes no day selectable — the grid is visible and inert. That
  is a caller bug, and it fails safely instead of throwing from render.

## Clearing

`clearable` adds a `Clear` action to the surface while a day is selected. It
reports `null`, closes the popover and returns focus to the trigger, matching the
selection path.

```tsx
<DatePicker clearable value={due} onValueChange={setDue}>
  <DatePickerTrigger label="Due date" />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

The action is opt-in so an existing field does not gain a row; the caller still
owns `null` as the "no date" state.

## Labels

Every string the calendar paints can come from the caller: month names, weekday
headings, the suffix on a day that cannot be chosen, the previous/next month and
year control names, the month-chooser control, its back control and the `Clear`
text. The defaults are English.

```tsx
<DatePicker
  labels={{
    months: ["jan", "feb", "mar", "apr", "mai", "jun", "jul", "aug", "sep", "okt", "nov", "des"],
    weekdays: ["So", "Mo", "Di", "Mi", "Do", "Fr", "Sa"],
    unavailable: "nicht verfügbar",
    previousMonth: "Vorheriger Monat",
    nextMonth: "Nächster Monat",
    previousYear: "Vorheriges Jahr",
    nextYear: "Nächstes Jahr",
    chooseMonth: "Monat wählen",
    showDays: "Tage anzeigen",
    clear: "Löschen",
  }}
>
  <DatePickerTrigger label="Fällig am" />
  <DatePickerContent>
    <DatePickerCalendar />
  </DatePickerContent>
</DatePicker>;
```

- The prop is **partial**: one overridden field keeps every other default.
- This is **not a locale**. Nothing looks a locale up, so the strings are exactly
  the ones you pass — feed them from your own message catalogue.
- `months` is January-first and `weekdays` is Sunday-first, whatever
  `weekStartsOn` displays; the grid rotates the headings.
- Only the **strings** are configurable. The shape (`October 2026`,
  `17 October 2026`) and the stored ISO value do not change, and weekday
  headings may repeat (initials such as `S M T W T F S` are fine) because they
  are keyed by column, not by text.

## Keyboard

| Key | Result |
| ---- | ------ |
| `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` | one day back/forward, crossing month boundaries, clamped to `min`/`max` |
| `ArrowUp` / `ArrowDown` | one week back/forward, clamped to `min`/`max` |
| `Home` / `End` | the week's first/last day, using `weekStartsOn`, clamped |
| `PageUp` / `PageDown` | previous/next month, clamped to the month's last day and the bounds |
| `Enter` / `Space` | selects the focused day, updates the trigger, closes and returns focus |
| `Escape` | closes and returns focus to the trigger |
| `Tab` | leaves the popover — it is not a trap |

One cell is the tab stop (the selected day, else today clamped to `min`/`max`); the
arrow keys move real focus, so the highlight and the focus are the same cell. An
adjacent-month day is a real cell but never adds a second tab stop. A no-value
open therefore lands on today — or on the first selectable day when today is out
of range — never on the month's first day.

In the month chooser the focused month is its one tab stop:

| Key | Result |
| ---- | ------ |
| `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight` | previous/next available month, without crossing into another year |
| `ArrowUp` / `ArrowDown` | three available months back/forward |
| `Home` / `End` | the first/last available month |
| `Enter` / `Space` | chooses the month, keeps the day-of-month and returns to the grid |
| `Escape` | closes the popover — the chooser adds no second dismiss layer |

## API

`CalendarLabels` is `{ months: ReadonlyArray<string>; weekdays:
ReadonlyArray<string>; unavailable: string; previousMonth: string;
nextMonth: string; previousYear: string; nextYear: string; chooseMonth: string;
showDays: string; clear: string }`. Every part merges `style` last where it takes one.

| Part | Renders | Props |
| ---- | ------- | ----- |
| `DatePicker` | provider (no host element) | `value?: string \| null`, `defaultValue?: string \| null`, `onValueChange?: (value: string \| null) => void`, `weekStartsOn?: 0 \| 1` (default `1`), `min?: string`, `max?: string`, `clearable?: boolean` (default `false`), `disabled?: boolean`, `today?: string`, `labels?: Partial<CalendarLabels>` |
| `DatePickerTrigger` | `div` (`role="button"`, `aria-expanded`) | the button's `variant`, `size` and `disabled`, plus `placeholder?: string` (default `"Pick a date"`), `label?: string`, `testId?: string` (default `"date-picker-trigger"`) |
| `DatePickerContent` | anchored surface | `side?: "top" \| "right" \| "bottom" \| "left"` (default `"bottom"`), `align?: "start" \| "center" \| "end"` (default `"start"`), `sideOffset?: number` (default `6`) |
| `DatePickerCalendar` | `div` (`role="grid"`) | `label?: string` (default `"Calendar"`) |

## Theming

The trigger paints `colors.background`, a `borderWidth.thin` border in
`colors.borderStrong` and `radius.md`. The surface paints `colors.popover` with
`colors.border` and `radius.md`. The selected day is `colors.primary` on
`colors.primaryForeground`; today keeps a `colors.primary` outline; hover uses
`colors.hover`; an unavailable day uses `colors.mutedForeground` and no hover; the
focus ring is `colors.ring`. The month chooser reuses those roles: the viewed
month is `colors.primary`, an unavailable month is `colors.mutedForeground`, and
its focused cell paints the same ring.

## Accessibility and limitations

Disabled controls announce **Disabled** through `aria-description`; 0.10.0 has no
`aria-disabled` semantics (plan 516).

- The grid is `role="grid"` with `role="row"` and `role="gridcell"`; a cell's
  `aria-label` is the full day (`"17 October 2026"`) and the chosen cell carries
  `aria-selected`. An unavailable day's name ends in `, unavailable`.
- The month chooser is a second `role="grid"` named `Choose month`; a cell's
  `aria-label` is `"October 2026"`, the viewed month carries `aria-selected`, and
  the same unavailable suffix applies. The year chevrons are ordinary buttons
  named `Previous year`/`Next year`.
- **No `aria-current`** in `@gpuix/native@0.10.0`: today is marked by a border,
  not by an attribute.
- **No `aria-disabled`** in `@gpuix/native@0.10.0`: an unavailable day leaves the
  tab order, does not activate, and is named as unavailable instead of carrying a
  disabled state.
- **`min` after `max`** makes the grid inert (see Bounds) rather than throwing.
- **Labels are the caller's.** The defaults are English; `labels` supplies the
  month names, weekday headings, unavailable suffix, the two month controls, the
  two year controls, the chooser's open/back names and `Clear`. There is no
  locale lookup and no `Intl` call, and the display *shape* is fixed (see Labels).
- **The chooser shows one year at a time.** There is no decade list, so an
  arbitrarily distant year takes one press per year.
- **No range selection** in v1.
- **No time of day.** A day and a time are separate values; compose **Time
  picker** beside this control when a form needs both. Folding them together
  would force a timestamp on every consumer that only needs one half.
- The calendar uses `DateTime` from Effect; there is no `Date` or `Intl` in
  `date-picker/calendar.ts`. The only clock read is `DateTime.nowUnsafe` for the
  default "today" — pass the `today` prop to make a test or a server boundary
  deterministic.

- Focus cues are seeded by `autoFocus`; the runtime does not reliably deliver
  React `focus`/`blur`, so a ring may not paint when focus moves by code
  (plans 390/603/605).
